Starts the firmware and its audio. A break plays on track 1 when you press PLAY.

Load your own loops

Drop a WAV on a slot, or tap one to pick a file. It's converted the way the Felucca editor does it (mono, 22.05 kHz, IMA ADPCM, up to 7.4 s). SHARD finds its tempo, so PLAY keeps it in time with the song. If the selected track plays SHARD, it switches to the slot.

Playing it

Knobs
Drag up or down, scroll, or focus one and use the arrow keys.
Buttons
Hold them as on the device: a held FX, GLO, SCL, EDIT or REC opens its layer.
Keyboard
A to ' play C to F. Space is PLAY, Esc is HOME, [ and ] are OCT- and OCT+.
SHARD
PRESETS picks SHARD after DRUM. EDIT steps through EDIT 1, EDIT 2, SLICES and SLICE FX.
Tempo
On the device it's on SLICE FX: turn KNOB 1 past the last slice to BUS, then BPM. AUTO is the tempo found.
